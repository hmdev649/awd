---
title: "A consistent look across the site, and picking several states at once"
date: 2026-11-16
kind: update
summary: "Text boxes, dropdowns, pills and page titles now match on every page, and the directory can filter by more than one state or province."
---
- The directory's State / Province filter now lets you pick more than one; the choice is kept in the page address so filtered views can be shared.
- Page titles are the same size on every page, including the homepage, and no bigger than the site name.
- Text boxes and dropdowns are the same height everywhere, the form dropdowns use the same arrow as the directory filters, and every text box shows the same outline when selected.
- Pills, small labels, the Contact page cards and the "Details confirmed by the brand" mark on brand pages now match the rest of the site.
- On phones, the states page shows two states per row.
- The rules behind the look are written down in [STYLE.md](https://github.com/hmdev649/awd/blob/main/STYLE.md), so future changes keep to them.

Added Bulova (New York), Movado Group (Paramus, New Jersey) and Invicta (Hollywood, Florida). All three have long histories and are based in the US today, so they fit the directory's rule of listing businesses based in America, whoever owns them and wherever their watches are made.

The Resources page has a new section on American watchmaking history, linking to the Pocket Watch Database's list of historic American watch manufacturers, the NAWCC's company records and a free 1888 history of American watch factories. We link to those lists instead of keeping our own list of closed companies.

Open question: the historic Elgin, Gruen and Waltham names are reportedly held by M.Z. Berger & Co. of New York, but none has its own website and ownership couldn't be confirmed, so they aren't listed yet.

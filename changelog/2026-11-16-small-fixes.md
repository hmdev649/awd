---
title: "A round of small fixes"
date: 2026-11-16
kind: update
summary: "A simpler directory filter bar, a shorter header, a new tagline and a clearer mark for brand-confirmed details."
---
- The directory no longer filters by founding year; sorting by oldest or newest still uses it. The remaining filters line up, and their dropdown arrows are centered.
- The header links to Directory, Map and About. States and provinces are still linked from the footer, the directory and the homepage.
- The tagline is now "A free, volunteer-run guide to watchmaking businesses based in America," and the homepage headline is smaller.
- The mark for "Details confirmed by the brand" is now a filled green badge, and it stays visible on phones when a long name is cut off.
- The "Made in America" guide moved to the Explore column of the footer, and its list of common wording is easier to read.

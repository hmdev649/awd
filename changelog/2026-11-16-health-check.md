---
title: "A weekly check that every listing's website still works"
date: 2026-11-16
kind: update
summary: "An automatic weekly check now flags listings whose websites have stopped working or moved, and brand pages can show when we last checked a listing."
---
- Every Monday an automatic check visits each listing's website and source links and reports the ones that don't answer, look parked, or now lead to a different site. It only reports: a person looks at each one before any listing changes.
- Listings can now carry a "Last checked" date, shown at the foot of the brand page. It is the date we last looked the listing over against the business's own website and found it still right. It is separate from "Last updated" and from "Details confirmed by the brand".
- Brand pages have a "report a problem" link, and the [Update a listing](/update/) form asks what the message is about, including whether a business has closed.

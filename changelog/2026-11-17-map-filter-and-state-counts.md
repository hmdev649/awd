---
title: "Filter the map by company type; clearer state counts"
date: 2026-11-17
kind: update
summary: "The map can now show one company type at a time, and state counts no longer include D.C., Puerto Rico and Canadian provinces as states."
---
- The map has a row of filters above it: all listings, or only watch brands, strap makers, accessories, components, tools or restoration.
- The homepage and the Browse by state page used to say "47 states". That number included Washington, D.C., Puerto Rico and six Canadian provinces. They now say 39 states, plus D.C., Puerto Rico and 6 Canadian provinces.
- We rechecked the counts on the state pages and the map: every listing with a location is counted once.

---
title: "A new product type: Accessories"
date: 2026-11-14
kind: decision
status: decided
summary: "Watch winders, boxes, rolls and similar goods get their own product type instead of being filed under Straps or Components."
---
**Decision.** Add "Accessories" to the product types, alongside Watches, Straps, Components, Tools and Restoration.

**Options considered.**

- File accessories under Components or Straps.
- A separate Accessories type (chosen).

**Why.** Winders, cases and watch rolls aren't parts of a watch or straps, and people looking for them should be able to filter for them.

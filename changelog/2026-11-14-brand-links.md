**Question.** Should the directory show how American watch companies connect: which suppliers serve which brands, and which brands have worked together?

**Options under consideration.**

- Links that both companies have stated publicly, each with a source, shown on both listings.
- Links only when the companies confirm them to us.
- No links for now.

**What matters.** One aim of the directory is helping American watch businesses find each other, and readers want to see how the industry fits together. Many supplier relationships are private, and some companies may not want them published, so any link needs a public source or the companies' agreement.

---
title: "Forms accept web addresses without https://"
date: 2026-11-15
kind: update
summary: "The Suggest a brand and Update a listing forms now accept web addresses like www.example.com, and Fossil is listed."
---
The website field on the [Suggest a brand](/suggest/) form, and the source link on the [Update a listing](/update/) form, used to reject an address typed without `https://`, so the form wouldn't send. Both now accept addresses typed any way and add `https://` when needed. We also added [Fossil](/brands/fossil/), based in Richardson, Texas, after the company asked to be listed.

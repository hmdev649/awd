title: "Listings need a business we can find; closed brands stay, with a label"
date: 2026-11-08
kind: decision
status: decided
summary: "We removed listings we couldn't locate or that aren't based in North America, and label brands that have closed instead of deleting them."
---
**Decision.** A listing needs a business we can identify, based in the United States or Canada. Brands that have stopped trading stay listed, with a "No longer trading", "Defunct" or "Coming soon" label.

**Options considered.**

- Keep every name that had ever been suggested.
- Delete brands that have closed.
- Keep closed brands, with a status label (chosen).

**Why.** A listing with no location, website or contact helps no one. Closed and historic brands are still part of the story of watchmaking here and people look them up, so they stay with a clear label. Fourteen listings were removed. Any of them can come back once we find where they are based, as Crown & Buckle did on November 14.

---
title: "“Verified by the brand” is now “Details confirmed by the brand”"
date: 2026-11-14
kind: update
summary: "We renamed the mark so it isn't mistaken for a check of a brand's manufacturing claims."
---
The mark means a brand confirmed the details on its listing. It doesn't mean anyone has checked where its products are made, which the directory doesn't do (see [how the directory handles made-in claims](/made-in-america/)). The new name says that more plainly. The filter in the directory has the same new name.

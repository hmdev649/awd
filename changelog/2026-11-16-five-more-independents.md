- Added Roysdon Watch Co. (Texas) with a "No longer trading" label, because its website says it is closed for now.
- Jacob & Co. also has a base in Geneva, and ZLTD Watches has a co-founder in Singapore. Both listings say so.
- Fernando Ronzon sells through social media and has no website; his listing gives Canada without a city until we can confirm one.

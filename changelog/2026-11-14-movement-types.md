**Question.** Should listings say which kinds of movements a brand uses, so the directory can be filtered by them?

**Options under consideration.**

- Broad tags from the brand's own product pages: mechanical, quartz, or both.
- Finer tags: automatic, hand-wound, quartz, solar and so on.
- No tags: leave it to the brand's description.

**What matters.** It was an early request from readers and was on our to-do list. Tags would come from what brands list on their own sites, and would need updating as their range changes.

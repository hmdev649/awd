- Most of the new logos are for the businesses added on November 16.
- 11 more were added on November 18 after a second look at sites that load their logos with JavaScript.
- 6 more came from businesses' Instagram profile pictures, where the picture is a logo rather than a photo of a person.
- 7 listings still have no logo, usually because the business's site doesn't show one we could use. Any business can send us its logo through the update form.

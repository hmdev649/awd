**Decision.** For now, we decide case by case whether a company with bases in both North America and another country counts as based in America. Listings for these companies give the North American city and mention the other location in the description. This answers the open question from November 14. The rule from November 16 still stands: a business whose workshop is in the US or Canada is listed.

**Options considered.**

- List any company that names a US or Canadian city as its home.
- List a company only if its main headquarters is in the US or Canada.
- Decide case by case (chosen, for now).

**Why.** Only a handful of businesses fall into this group so far, and they differ a lot, from a company with a long-standing New York flagship and a Geneva base to two co-founders working in different countries. Judging each one is simpler than picking a rule too early. We may set a single rule once there are more examples.

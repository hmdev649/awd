---
We added three brands suggested by readers: [Burnt Creek Watch Company](/brands/burnt-creek-watch-company/) (Bismarck, North Dakota), [Farr & Swit](/brands/farr-and-swit/) (Elmhurst, Illinois) and [Wolf Creek Watch Company](/brands/wolf-creek-watch-company/) (Duluth, Minnesota).

Directory cards and map labels used to show only a city and a state or province code, so "Brandon, MB" and "Brandon, MN" looked alike to anyone unfamiliar with the codes. Canadian listings now end in "Canada".

---
title: "We report what brands say about where things are made; we don't judge it"
date: 2026-11-09
kind: decision
status: decided
summary: "The directory makes no claims of its own about what is or isn't American made. It reports each brand's own claims, attributed to the brand."
---
**Decision.** Anything about where a product is designed, made or assembled is written as the brand's own claim ("the brand says its watches are assembled in Texas"), ideally with a link to where it says so. We don't add our own judgments, and we don't repeat press claims as fact.

**Options considered.**

- Our own made-in-America rating for each brand.
- Ratings from a third party, credited to them.
- Only what each brand itself claims (chosen).

**Why.** We're volunteers and can't inspect factories or supply chains. Our own judgments would invite disputes we can't settle. 74 brand descriptions were reworded to say whose claim each one is.

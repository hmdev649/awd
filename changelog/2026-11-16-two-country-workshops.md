---
title: "Listing a business with bases in two countries"
date: 2026-11-16
kind: decision
status: decided
summary: "A business that presents itself as based both in North America and abroad can be listed when its workshop is in the US or Canada."
---
**Decision.** We list a business that names a city abroad alongside a North American one, as long as its workshop is in the US or Canada. The listing gives the North American city, and its description mentions the other location.

**Options considered.** List it under its North American city, or leave it out because it is also based outside North America.

**Why.** The directory covers businesses based in America. A workshop here is a real base, and the description makes the second location clear to readers.

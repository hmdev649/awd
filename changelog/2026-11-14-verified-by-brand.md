---
title: "Marking listings the brand has confirmed"
date: 2026-11-14
kind: decision
status: decided
summary: "Listings whose details the brand confirmed to us show a \"Verified by the brand\" mark and the date, and the directory can show only those."
---
**Decision.** When a brand confirms its details through our form or by email, its listing shows a "Verified by the brand" mark with the date. The directory has a filter for verified listings.

**Why.** Most listings are researched from public sources. The mark tells readers which details came from the brand itself and how recently. Ten brands that confirmed their details in late 2025 have it now.

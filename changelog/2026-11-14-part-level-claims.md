summary: "Brand descriptions include what brands say about where their movements, cases and other parts are made. We're weighing whether repeating those claims, even credited to the brand, does more harm than good."
---
**Question.** Should listings repeat what brands say about where individual parts are made, given that we can't check those claims?

**Options under consideration.**

- Keep part-level claims in descriptions, credited to the brand (what the site does today).
- Move them into a structured record for each brand, with a link to the brand's own statement for every claim.
- Leave part-level detail out, and link to the brand's own page about how its watches are made.

**What matters.** Readers want to know where watches are made, and brands that are open about their supply chains deserve credit for it. But some brands' claims go further than the facts, and repeating a claim on a directory can lend it weight even when it's credited to the brand. Brands that are candid about imported parts shouldn't look less American than brands that say less. It also affects the [made-in badge question](#made-in-badge).

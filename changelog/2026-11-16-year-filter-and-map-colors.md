---
title: "Founding-year filter back, US and Canada apart on the map"
date: 2026-11-16
kind: update
summary: "The directory can filter by founding year again, the map colors and counts US and Canadian businesses separately, and brand pages can show up to three photos."
---
- The directory has a Founded filter again, as a dropdown where you can pick more than one period. The Sort dropdown moved out of the filter panel onto the line that shows how many listings match.
- The state filter is now simply called "State" across the site. It still includes Canadian provinces.
- On the map, United States businesses are dark blue-grey and Canadian ones brass. The two countries are grouped separately, so a cluster's count never mixes them. Pins placed only at state level are now hollow.
- The round AWD mark now appears beside the site name in the header.
- On the homepage, "brands, watchmakers & suppliers" links to the directory.
- "Made in America" is now the first link in the footer's Explore column.
- Brand pages can show a gallery of up to three photos, such as watches, key people or how things are made.
- The FAQs now say the directory is updated "a few times a month", and explain that brands can confirm their details with us, shown by the green tick mark.

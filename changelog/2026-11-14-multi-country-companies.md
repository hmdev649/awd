**Question.** Some companies have a US office alongside offices or headquarters in other countries. When are they "based in America"?

**Options under consideration.**

- List any company with a US office.
- List a company only if its main headquarters is in America.
- List a company if its leadership or main office is here, or it describes itself as an American company.

**What matters.** The directory is for businesses based here, not their US sales offices. The test should rely on what the company itself publishes.

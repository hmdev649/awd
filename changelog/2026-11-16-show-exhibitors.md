title: "Nine businesses from the Windup and Toronto watch shows"
date: 2026-11-16
kind: update
summary: "We compared the 2026 exhibitor lists of the Windup Watch Fair and the Toronto Timepiece Show with the directory and added nine US and Canadian businesses that were missing."
---
- From the Windup Watch Fair: DeMarco Watch Co. (Boston), M. Ryan Watch Designs (Chatham, New Jersey), Ocean Crawler (Charlotte, North Carolina), Prevail Watches (Florida) and Papar Watch Co. (Massachusetts).
- From the Toronto Timepiece Show: Mount West Watch Co. (Utah), Slomo (Toronto), Beauregard (Montreal) and Charles Simon (Montreal), which makes watch cases and other accessories.
- Most other exhibitors at both shows were already listed or are based outside North America. Retailers, repairers and businesses that don't make watch products were left out.

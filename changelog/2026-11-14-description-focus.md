**Question.** What should a brand's short description on its listing be about?

**Options under consideration.**

- Mostly the brand's story, design and mission, with at most a short, credited line on where things are made.
- Story and made-in detail together, as most descriptions are now.
- Story only, with made-in detail kept to a separate field or the badge, if we add one.

**What matters.** Descriptions should help someone decide whether a brand is worth a closer look, and stay accurate as brands change. Made-in claims change more often than a brand's story and are harder to check.

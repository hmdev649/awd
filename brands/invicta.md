Invicta Watch Group is based in Hollywood, Florida. The Invicta name dates to 1837, when Raphael Picard founded the brand in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland. In 1991 an American investment group led by Eyal Lalo acquired it and set up the Invicta Watch Company of America in Florida. The group also owns the Swiss brand Glycine.

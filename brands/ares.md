ARES Watch Company is a tool-watch brand in Stanwood, Washington. Its lineup centers on rugged divers such as the Diver-1, often with Cerakote-coated cases.

The brand has made collaborations and limited editions with partners including author Jack Carr and Black Rifle Coffee Company. It also sells straps and runs its own service and warranty program.

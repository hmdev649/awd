Sangin Instruments is an El Cajon, California, brand founded in 2016. It makes purpose-built tool watches for people who serve in demanding environments, including military personnel. Models include the Kingmaker, Hydra, Atlas, Kinetic, Neptune and Overlord, and it also makes knives and gear.

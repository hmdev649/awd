Pelton is a Detroit watch brand founded by machinist and designer Deni Mesanovic. It makes its own cases, crowns and bracelets. Its watches include the Sector. The 10-pointed star in its logo comes from the coat of arms of Prijedor, Bosnia, where Mesanovic was born.

Jacob & Co. is a watch and jewelry house founded in New York in 1986 by Jacob Arabo. Its flagship is on East 57th Street in Manhattan, and it also has a base in Geneva, Switzerland. It is known for elaborate complications and high jewelry watches, such as the Astronomia and the Epic X.

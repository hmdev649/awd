Toledano & Chan is a watch company with its business address in New York City, founded by artist Phil Toledano and designer Alfred Chan, who say they work from opposite sides of the world. Its shaped watches, starting with the B/1, take their cues from brutalist architecture, with an asymmetrical sapphire crystal and an integrated case and bracelet.

The brand says the B/1 Dazzle is made in Switzerland, with a Swiss Sellita movement.

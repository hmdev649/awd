FLEUX is a Toronto brand founded in 2021 that makes modern skin-diver watches, a slim style of dive watch, inspired by designs from the 1960s and 1970s. Its founder traces his love of watches to watching his grandmother wind hers every day. The brand won a 2023 Good Design Award from the Chicago Athenaeum.

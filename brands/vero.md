VERO Watch Company was founded in Portland, Oregon, in 2015 to show that well-made watches don't have to be expensive or boring. It makes adventure watches inspired by nature, such as the Workhorse and the Tide Tracker. It has also made collaborations with Smokey Bear and Realtree.

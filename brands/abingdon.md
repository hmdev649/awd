Abingdon Co. makes tool watches for women, aimed at pilots, scuba divers, race car drivers, mechanics and travelers. The company started in 2006, when pilot Abingdon Mullin heard fellow women pilots and mechanics asking for a fully functional aviator's watch that suited them.

According to the brand, every model has at least four functions besides telling the time, such as dive bezels, chronographs or extra time zones. It is based in Las Vegas.

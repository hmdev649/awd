Detroit Watch Company was founded in 2013. It designs watches and hand-assembles them in small batches in its Michigan studio, using Swiss automatic movements. Each watch is also adjusted and tested there. Its designs draw on Detroit's history, including the city's French founding by Antoine de la Mothe Cadillac.

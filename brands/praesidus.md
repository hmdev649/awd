Praesidus is a Phoenix brand founded in 2019 that makes WWII-inspired field watches, designed with veterans and assembled in the USA. Its first watch was a near-exact replica of the A-11 that veteran Tom Rice lost in the ocean when he deployed into Normandy on D-Day.

It also makes officially licensed U.S. Army watches and editions built with material from a WWII Willys MB jeep.

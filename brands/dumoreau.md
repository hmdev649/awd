Dumoreau is a watch brand founded in 2020 in Huntington Beach, California, by architect and product designer Carlo Aiello. Its current model is a monopusher chronograph limited to 150 pieces, with panda and reverse panda dials.

The brand says its watches are designed in California and made in Switzerland, and that they ship from its workshop in Huntington Beach.

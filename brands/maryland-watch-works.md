Maryland Watch Works was a Hagerstown, Maryland, company co-founded by Pete Brown (of Hager Watches) and watchmaker Eugene Stohlman. It said it assembled its 7081 movement, based on the ETA 2834, in Hagerstown. It also ran a state-approved watchmaking apprenticeship. In 2025 the business was reported to have been acquired by Tsao Baltimore, and its website now redirects there. It is no longer trading under its own name.

Deep Blue was founded in New York City in 2007 and makes professional-grade dive watches. Over the years its collections have included water resistance from 300 to 3,000 meters, built-in depth meters, ceramic bezels, tritium lighting and helium-release valves.

The brand says its watches are worn by divers, military personnel and first responders.

Weiss Watch Company was founded by watchmaker Cameron Weiss in 2013 and is now an independent atelier in Nashville. It says it makes limited-edition mechanical watches by hand, drawing on the discipline of Swiss watchmaking. The brand's motto is restoring prestige to American watchmaking.

Cincinnati Watch Company designs and assembles watches in Cincinnati, Ohio. Its founders, Rick Bell and Mark Stegman, first started Build Your Own Watch, which let hobbyists assemble and case their own watches.

Its collections, including Union Terminal and Cincinnatus, take their names from Cincinnati landmarks and history. It also makes enamel-dial pieces and offers watch repair.

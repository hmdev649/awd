Havid Nagan is an independent brand that says it designs its watches in Los Angeles and has them made in Switzerland. Its designs draw on 18th- and 19th-century motifs and traditional techniques such as guilloché, for example the Grain d'Orge pattern on the HN-00-01's dial. Its collections are the HN and Classic lines, with an Origins line coming.

Havid Nagan told us its watches are designed in America and made in Switzerland, and that it fits straps and bracelets and packs its watches in the US.

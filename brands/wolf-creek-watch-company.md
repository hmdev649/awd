Wolf Creek Watch Company is a Duluth, Minnesota, brand started by Michael Johansen in 2020 to make simple, practical mechanical watches. Its first watch was the Voyageur Mechanical, with a bronze case and an ETA 2801 hand-wound movement, followed by the North Star Automatic in stainless steel with a Sellita movement. The brand describes its watches as "Duluth designed" and "Swiss made."

Ocean Crawler is a dive watch brand based in Charlotte, North Carolina. Its collections include the Core Diver, Dream Diver, Champion Diver and Field Crawler, and it also sells straps and bracelets. It has exhibited at the Windup Watch Fair.

The brand says each watch "is assembled in the United States by a single watchmaker from start to finish", using Swiss automatic movements.

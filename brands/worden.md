Worden Watch Studio is a Canadian watch brand run by industrial designer Tyler Worden, based in Guelph, Ontario. Each release is built around a theme, such as a feeling, experience or idea, which shapes its colors, materials and design. Releases include Bubble Gum and Night Drift.

ZLTD Watches was founded by Z.L. Toh, from Singapore, and watchmaker Raf Dzwonek, from Canada, who is based in Calgary. Its releases include Series 1, Series 1.5, Series 2 and a Series 2 collaboration with Oan.Gio.

The brand says it develops its own complication modifications and makes high-precision movement components in house, on CNC machines it also builds, with hand finishing in its workshop.

M. Ryan Watch Designs is a watch brand based in Chatham, New Jersey. Its first model, the Layne (Series No. 1), is a mechanical field-style watch, on pre-order with deliveries expected in February 2027. The brand says it will donate part of every sale to epilepsy and rare-disease causes. It has exhibited at the Windup Watch Fair.

The brand says its watches are "Designed & Assembled in the USA".

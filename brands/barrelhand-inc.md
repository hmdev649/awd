Barrelhand is a Grass Valley, California, company that makes timepieces and objects with space exploration in mind. Its watch, the Monolith, is described as built for space. The company's Memory Disc project aims to preserve human artifacts.

Barrelhand told us its watches are designed in America, that some movement parts, crystals and straps are made here, and that it cases its watches, fits their straps and does all testing and prototyping in the US.

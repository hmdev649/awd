Vaer was founded in 2016 to build the ideal everyday watch, one you can wear from the ocean to the office. Its range includes field, dive, chronograph and GMT watches with high water resistance and two interchangeable straps. The brand says some models are assembled in the USA and that it sources many parts there. It is based in Venice Beach, California.

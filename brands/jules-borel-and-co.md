Jules Borel & Co. began when the Borel family sold watch parts door to door across the United States, and it settled in Kansas City in 1920. Today it supplies tools and equipment to watchmakers and jewelers, is the exclusive North American distributor for Witschi, and sells watchmaker's benches it lists as made in the USA.

Celeste Watch Company is a woman-owned watch company in Springfield, Oregon, that specializes in hand-decorated dials. Its quartz and automatic watches feature abalone, mother-of-pearl, wood and enamel dials, with themes from sea life and wildlife to zodiac signs. It also makes custom pet portraits and other commissions.

The company works with enamel artists, runs a watchmaking program for them, and has a boutique on Main Street.

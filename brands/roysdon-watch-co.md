Roysdon Watch Co. is the Texas workshop of Paul F. Roysdon, who makes very small runs of high-end watches. Independentwatchmaking.com reports that he makes most movement parts himself and casts cases in his own gold alloy. The brand's site says its watches are "proudly USA MADE".

As of November 2026 the brand's website says it is "Closed for now".

Haven Watch Co. is a Midwestern brand in Fort Wayne, Indiana. The brand says its in-house watchmakers assemble, adjust, regulate and repair its watches in the US, and that some components are also made in the US. It says the movements are Swiss.

Its watches aim to be well made and everyday-tough, without needing to be babied.

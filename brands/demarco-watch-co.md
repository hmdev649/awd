DeMarco Watch Co. is a Boston watch brand that takes its design cues from Italian watchmaking. Its range includes the Ironside 200 automatic dive watch and a chronograph with a scalloped bezel. It has exhibited at the Windup Watch Fair.

The brand says its watches are "designed & assembled in Boston". Its FAQ adds that they are partially assembled, but not made, in the USA.

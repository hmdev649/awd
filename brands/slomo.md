Slomo is a Toronto watch brand started by art director Ben Moyle. Its first watch, the Glance, is an automatic built around an unusual way of reading the time, and was launched on Kickstarter in 2025. It has exhibited at the Toronto Timepiece Show.

The brand says its watches are designed in Toronto.

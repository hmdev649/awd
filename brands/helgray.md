Helgray is a watch brand founded by Nadim Elgarhy, with its business address in Beaconsfield, near Montreal. It launched on Kickstarter in April 2014 with watches inspired by vintage military designs, and the campaign became one of the most-funded in its category. Its collections are the Superfast, a racing-inspired chronograph; the Bomber, a pilot watch; and the Brigadier, inspired by World War II military watches. The brand says its watches use Swiss and Japanese movements, and they are made in small limited editions. Its website says they are designed in the UK.

Charles Simon makes leather watch accessories: watch rolls, cases and boxes, travel cases, stands and a watch winder, along with briefcases and other travel goods. It was started by two engineers who met while studying in Montreal. It has exhibited at the Toronto Timepiece Show.

The brand says each piece "begins and ends in our Montreal atelier".

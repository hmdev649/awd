Camp Watches is a Los Angeles brand founded in 2024. It designs its watches in LA and has them made in Switzerland.

Its signature is a deep lacquer dial built up from fifteen layers of Swiss enamelling lacquer, each oven-cured and hand-polished. The dials use typography of the brand's own design, and the watches run on a customized hand-wound mechanical movement.

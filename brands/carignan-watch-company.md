Carignan Watch Company is a watch repair and restoration shop in Belmont, New Hampshire. Denis Carignan started it in 1996, after years of repairing watches as a hobby.

The shop services luxury Swiss watches as well as antique and fusee watches, both American and European. It will make obsolete parts that can no longer be bought.

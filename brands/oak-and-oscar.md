Oak & Oscar is an independent watch company founded in Chicago in 2015. Its models include the Atwood Chronograph, the Olmsted and the Humboldt GMT, along with hand-painted dial pieces. It also makes straps and accessories, and sees customers by appointment.

Oak & Oscar told us its watches are designed in America. It says that, depending on the model, it has made movement parts, cases, dials and straps itself, though most of its watch parts come from Switzerland. It says its Chicago headquarters mainly fits straps and bracelets and cases movements with their dials and hands, and that it has also assembled movements there.

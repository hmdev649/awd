Tessé is a Toronto watch brand founded by designer Thomas Brissiaud. Its first watch, the Michel GMT, was inspired by his grandfather's watch from the 1970s and took nearly two years to develop. It has a cushion-shaped case and a Sellita GMT movement. The range now also includes The Architect. Tessé describes its watches as Swiss made. Brissiaud also wrote a book, *The Forgotten Innovations of 70s Watchmaking*.

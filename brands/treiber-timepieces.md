Treiber Timepieces is a San Francisco brand that makes watches inspired by classic cars, which it says are assembled by hand in California.

Treiber told us its watches are designed, assembled and tested in San Francisco with Swiss movements. It says it modifies and adjusts movements there, and that its straps and the finishing of some parts are done in the US.

Forstner began around 1920 in New Jersey as the Forstner Chain Corporation and was acquired by Jacoby Bender (JB Champion) in 1963. Today's Forstner reissues bracelets from the old catalogs, including the Komfit mesh, the Klip and the Beads of Rice.

Jack Mason was founded in 2015. It designs watches in Dallas, Texas, and says they are built in the United States by its own watchmakers. Its models include the Strat-o-timer GMT and the Canton Day-Date. The brand presents its work as part of the American watchmaking tradition of Hamilton, Elgin and Waltham.

Keaton Myrick is an independent watchmaker in central Oregon. He trained at the Lititz Watch Technicum in Pennsylvania and worked for Rolex before setting up his own restoration business. He specializes in high-end wrist and pocket watches and the restoration of rare timepieces. His best-known watch is the "1 in 30" chronometer, named for the number that will be made.

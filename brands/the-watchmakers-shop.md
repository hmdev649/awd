The Watchmaker's Shop is a watch service and repair business in the historic Grove Arcade in downtown Asheville, North Carolina. It has served western North Carolina since 2010. Every watch is handled by a certified watchmaker, and in 2017 the shop opened a secure service center. It also buys and sells pre-owned watches.

HOFFMAN is an American brand founded in New York in 2016 by Will Hoffman. It reimagines vintage sports watches, starting with a 1960s-style racing chronograph, and now makes the Racing 40 and Diver 40. The watches are produced in small numbers with carefully chosen partners.

Mount West Watch Co. is a new watch brand based in Utah, which says it makes "timepieces that are influenced by nature". Its first model, the Summit, has a dual unidirectional bezel; a second model, the Basecamp, is on pre-order. It has exhibited at the Toronto Timepiece Show.

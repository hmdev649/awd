Sarauer Horology is the workshop of WOSTEP-trained watchmaker Aaron Sarauer in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. Its first watch, the Ref. 119C, is a hand-wound watch made in a series of 29, with a guilloché dial. The brand describes its watches as hand made in Canada.

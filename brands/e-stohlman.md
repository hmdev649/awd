E. Stohlman Watch Co. is the brand of Eugene Stohlman, a third-generation watchmaker who learned the craft in his grandfather's and uncle's workshops and then trained formally. It is based in Hagerstown, Maryland. The brand makes classic-style watches, including small limited editions in materials such as sterling silver and jade.

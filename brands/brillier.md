Brillier is a watch brand in Mesa, Arizona. It says its watches are designed, assembled and tested in the United States, and that it uses as many American-made parts as it can source, with the goal of fully American-made watches.

Its collections, such as Airborne, Native, Railroad and Military, draw on American history.

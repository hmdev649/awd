Alterum Watch Co. is a Dunedin, Florida brand that calls itself a hybrid American-Swiss watch company: American design paired with Swiss watchmaking. Its design approach centers on simplicity, drawing on modern architecture and early industrial design.

Its best-known model is the Worldtimer, made in several city editions such as L.A. and N.Y.C.

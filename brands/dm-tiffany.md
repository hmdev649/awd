DM Tiffany is a self-taught independent watchmaker in Phoenix, Arizona, who began in 2018 with a benchtop milling machine and the goal of making his own watch case. He now makes a growing number of parts himself and has built his own machines, including a rose engine lathe for guilloché dials.

His watches are made in small series. Each one has its own guilloché pattern, finishing, coloring and strap.

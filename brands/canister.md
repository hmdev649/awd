Canister Watches was founded in Brandon, Manitoba, by Chris Lewington, an active-duty member of the Canadian Armed Forces with twenty years of watch collecting behind him. He designed the brand's first model, the Fieldmaster, at his kitchen table, and it took more than 18 months to design, prototype and produce.

The brand's aim is rugged, dependable tool watches without unnecessary extras.

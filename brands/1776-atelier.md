1776 Atelier is an independent watch company in Fulshear, Texas, founded by Jason Lu and Zach Smith. It aims at high-end mechanical watchmaking: the brand says its movements are designed from the ground up with their own bridge layouts, and that finishing such as hand beveling (anglage), perlage, black polishing and engraving is done by hand.

Each watch is individually numbered, and the brand describes its pieces as semi-bespoke.

Everest Bands makes rubber, leather and nylon straps fitted to Rolex, Tudor and Panerai cases, so owners can wear a strap without changing the watch. It says its straps are made in Switzerland.

Founder Michael DiMartini and co-founder David Barnes also started the watch brand Monta.

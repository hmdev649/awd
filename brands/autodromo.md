Autodromo is a New York brand founded in 2011 that makes watches inspired by motor racing. Its designs, such as the Monoposto, Group C and Turbo Sport, draw on vintage race cars and dashboard instruments with a minimalist, mid-century look.

Besides watches, it sells driving gloves, straps and apparel.

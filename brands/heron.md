Héron Watch Co. is a Montreal company that pairs neo-vintage design with modern watchmaking, aiming to capture the charm of 20th-century watches. Its collections include the Marinor diver, the Continental and the Mirabel GMT.

Héron has a showroom and also offers bespoke work, watch repair and servicing.

Farr & Swit is a watch brand from Elmhurst, Illinois, outside Chicago, started by three friends, Adam Hodge, Andrew Paetzold and Zach Switalski, who call themselves "watch guys." Its first watch, the Seaplane Automatic, launched in 2019. The range now includes the Wayfinder and playful retro digital watches such as the Mix Tape and VHS, which have been sold through the MoMA Design Store, along with straps and accessories.

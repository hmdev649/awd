Mark Carson is a Honolulu brand founded by a software engineer from Iowa who designed his first watch, the Ka La, for himself in 2009. The brand describes its watches as having a "Swiss heart, Hawaiian soul": Swiss movements in watches designed and assembled by the brand. Its main collection is the Kailua, which includes a GMT.

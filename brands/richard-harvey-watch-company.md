Richard-Harvey Watch Company is a veteran-owned watch brand in Sterling, Virginia, named after its founder's two grandfathers, both military veterans. Its first model, the Attitude, comes in two versions, the Patriot and the Blackout, and its range also includes the Lark field watch.

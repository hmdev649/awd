HALIOS is a small Vancouver watch company started in 2009 that makes dive and sport watches, including the Seaforth and Universa. It grew out of online dive-watch forums. The brand sells in pre-order batches.

It runs on three principles: a well-built watch you can take into the water, strong after-sales support, and cool design.

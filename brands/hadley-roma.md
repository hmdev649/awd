Hadley-Roma calls itself America's Watch Strap Company. It says it was founded in 1912 in Providence, Rhode Island, and now runs a 40,000 square foot factory and distribution center in Largo, Florida, where it makes products it describes as made in the USA. It also produces some lines overseas.

It makes leather, metal and specialty watch straps, as well as belts and accessories.

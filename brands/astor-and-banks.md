Astor + Banks is a Chicago watch brand founded in 2012 by a military veteran who has loved watches since childhood. It makes men's watches with mechanical and Swiss quartz movements, including the Fortitude Pro and Fortitude Lite.

The brand says all its watches are assembled in Chicago.

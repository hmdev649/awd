Papar Watch Co. is a small watch brand run by Josh and Emily Blank. According to Fratello, it started in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico, and now works from the Berkshire Mountains of Massachusetts. Its first watch was the Anillo GMT, with a sector dial and folded bezel; it has since added the Vuelte wandering-hour watch and the Cenote titanium and ceramic dive watch. It has exhibited at the Windup Watch Fair.

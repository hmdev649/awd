Typsim (pronounced "tip-sim") makes durable mechanical watches in classic, sport, dive and vintage styles. Its name comes from architects' shorthand: "typ" for typical and "sim" for similar. It began in 2005 as a design collective of three young architects in a Buffalo basement and became a watch company in 2019. It is now based in Seattle.

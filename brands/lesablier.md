Lesablier is a watch brand based in Washington, D.C., started in 2017 by a collector who began by building watches from salvaged parts. Its models have included the AVQ pilot's watch, the Sport Classic and Chronosport integrated-bracelet watches, and the LS-04 Travelgraph world-timer.

The brand says it makes fewer than 400 watches a year, in small runs of each color.

ADPT is a line of nylon watch straps from Worn & Wound, the watch publication and shop. Worn & Wound says its components and packaging are sourced and made entirely in the US. The straps follow a military design with a security band underneath, which keeps the watch on the wrist if a spring bar fails. They come in two-piece and single-pass versions.

Beauregard makes high-jewelry watches set with carved gemstones, such as the Dahlia, which has a central flying tourbillon, and the Ulysse, made with master watchmaker Vianney Halter. It was founded by Montreal-born lapidary artist Alexandre Beauregard. The brand signs itself "Genève Montréal"; the Calibrated Wrist describes its workshop in Montreal's Ville-Marie district. It has exhibited at the Toronto Timepiece Show.

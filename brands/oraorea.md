OraOrea is a watch brand in Brooklyn, New York, created by Zach Starr Weiss, a co-founder of Worn & Wound and the Windup Watch Fair. Its first model is the Coriolis, a pointer-date watch, and it also sells straps. The name comes from the Latin for "golden hour".

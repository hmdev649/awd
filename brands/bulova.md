Bulova was founded in New York City in 1875 by Joseph Bulova, an immigrant from Bohemia, and is still based in New York. In 1941 it aired the first television advertisement in the US, and its Accutron, introduced in 1960, kept time with a tuning fork instead of a balance wheel. Citizen Watch of Japan has owned Bulova since 2008.

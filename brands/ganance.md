Ganance is a Chicago company that makes the Heir, a health-tracking module that attaches to a regular watch or bracelet so the wearer can keep their own watch and still collect health data. Its founder, a first-generation Mexican-American, started the company after a health diagnosis, using a watch his brother gave him as the inspiration.

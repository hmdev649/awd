AW Labs is a one-person watchmaking studio in Fairfax, Virginia, founded by Mike Armstrong in 2025. Its first watch is the Origin Ref. 001.

The studio says it machines its cases, dials and main structure in house, in a Fairfax workshop on a mill Armstrong built himself, and that each watch is designed and built by him. It describes itself as "Independent American Watchmaking".

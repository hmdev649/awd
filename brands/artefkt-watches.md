Artefkt is an independent tool-watch brand founded in Detroit by Brandon Little. It makes titanium tool watches, with models such as the Baseline One, La Pointe and Canyonlands.

The brand says its watches are made with a global network of specialist partners, an approach it calls "Artefkt Made". The name is its take on "artifact", an object that shows human skill.

Lorier is a New York brand, founded in 2016, that makes affordable vintage-inspired watches. Its range includes the Neptune, a 39 mm diver with a Miyota 9039 movement and 200 m water resistance, and the Falcon, a 36 mm field watch. It also has a showroom.

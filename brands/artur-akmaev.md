Artur Akmaev is a Moscow-born engraver and independent watchmaker who has worked in Los Angeles since 2017. He is known for highly decorated, skeletonized watches with hand engraving and enamel. His themes draw on comic books, video games and East Asian art, in pieces such as Battle City and Rise of the Blue Dragon. He has also engraved movements for other makers, including J.N. Shapiro.

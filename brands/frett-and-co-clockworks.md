Frett & Co. Clockworks was founded in Little Rock, Arkansas, in March 2020 by Brendan and Kylie Frett. It restores vintage American pocket watch movements and converts them into wristwatches, and also takes custom conversion commissions.

The company says each of its timepieces is handcrafted on site by Brendan Frett.

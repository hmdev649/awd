Rpaige Watches is the Honolulu brand of Richard Paige, a fourth-generation watchmaker. He founded several California watch stores, including Paris 1925 in San Francisco, and founded TimeZone.com, a pioneering online community for watch enthusiasts, where he also ran an online watch-repair school.

Rpaige makes unique mechanical watches inspired by history and Art Deco design, and lets buyers configure their own watch.

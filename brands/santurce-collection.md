Santurce Collection was started in Santurce, Puerto Rico, by husband-and-wife team José and Anita. It began when José sold off some of his own watches to free up closet space. The brand designs its watches in Puerto Rico and assembles them in the USA. It makes divers, pilot watches, GMTs and field watches, and has collaborated with Vulcain.

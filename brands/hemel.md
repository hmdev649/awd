Hemel describes itself as Long Island's first watch microbrand, based in Nassau County, New York, and founded by Marvin Menke. It makes military-inspired mechanical watches with vintage styling, such as the hand-wound Sky Racer and the Air League and Air Defense models. Menke also founded the League of Microbrands, an online community for small watch brands.

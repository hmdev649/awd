Movado Group is based in Paramus, New Jersey. It began in the 1960s as the North American Watch Corporation, a watch distributor founded by Gedalio Grinberg, bought the Movado brand in 1983 and took the Movado Group name in 1996. The Movado brand itself was founded in 1881 in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland. The group also owns Concord, Ebel, Olivia Burton and MVMT, and makes watches under license for fashion brands.

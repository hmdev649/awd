Timex traces its history to 1854, when the Waterbury Clock Company was founded in Waterbury, Connecticut, to make timekeeping affordable. By 1901 its pocket watches sold for a dollar. Today Timex Group is based in Middlebury, Connecticut, and designs, makes and distributes watches under Timex and several other brands.
